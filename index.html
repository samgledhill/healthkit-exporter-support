My Health Export

Fast, reliable health data export for iOS

🚀 Getting Started

  1. Open the app and grant Health app permissions for the data types you want to export
  2. Select your metrics from the list (free users can export 8 core metrics; Pro unlocks 26 more)
  3. Choose your date range (free: last 7 days; Pro: any dates)
  4. Pick your format: CSV (for spreadsheets like Excel or Google Sheets) or JSON (for scripts and tools)
  5. Tap Export and share via email, AirDrop, or save to the Files app

📱 Free vs Pro Features

✅ Free (No Purchase Required)

Metrics: Steps, Heart Rate, Weight (most recent), Sleep (total and sleep stages: Core, REM, Deep, Asleep)

Date Range: Last 7 days

Export Formats: CSV and JSON (summary format: one row per day)

Export Limit: Up to 10 exports per day

⭐ Pro (One-Time In-App Purchase)

Date Range: Any start and end date – export your full history or specific periods

26 Additional Metrics:

  • Activity & Energy: Walking/Running Distance, Cycling Distance, Swimming Distance, Flights Climbed, Exercise Minutes, Total Energy (active + resting), Resting Energy
  • Heart & Breathing: Resting Heart Rate, Walking Heart Rate Average, HRV (SDNN), Respiratory Rate, VO2 Max, Blood Oxygen
  • Body Composition: Body Fat %, Lean Body Mass, BMI, Body Temperature (manual and average), Wrist Temperature (overnight from Apple Watch)
  • Blood: Blood Pressure (systolic and diastolic, most recent or daily average), Blood Glucose (most recent or daily average)
  • Sleep (iOS 18+): Breathing Disturbances (overnight), Sleep Apnea Events
  • Mindfulness & Hearing: Mindful Minutes, Environmental Audio (dB), Headphone Audio (dB)

Raw Export: Tick "Export as Raw data" on any metric to get a second file with every individual reading timestamped, including source device/app

Unlimited Exports: No daily limit

📊 Export Formats Explained

Summary Export (Free & Pro)

One row per day for each metric you select. Perfect for tracking trends over time in a spreadsheet.

Example columns: Date, Steps, Heart Rate (bpm), Weight (kg), Sleep (hours)

Raw Export (Pro Only)

Every individual reading with timestamp, value, unit and source device/app. Tick "Export as Raw data" for any metric to get both summary and raw files.

Example columns: Timestamp, Date, Time, Metric, Value, Unit, Source, Source Bundle ID

Note: Raw export files can be very large if you export years of data for metrics recorded frequently (like heart rate).

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Which metrics require iOS 18?
A: Breathing Disturbances (overnight) and Sleep Apnea Events are only available on iOS 18 and later with a compatible Apple Watch.
Q: Can I restore my Pro purchase on another device?
A: Yes! Open the app, go to Manage Purchases, and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID.
Q: Why is my export missing recent data?
A: Make sure your Apple Watch or other devices have synced with the Health app. Open the Health app to trigger a sync, then try exporting again.
Q: What's the difference between "most recent" and "average" for weight, blood pressure and blood glucose?
A: "Most recent" gives you the last recorded value for each day. "Average" (Pro only for BP and glucose) calculates the daily average if you take multiple readings per day.
Q: Can I export workout data?
A: Not yet. The app currently exports health metrics like steps, heart rate and distance, but not structured workout sessions.
Q: What does "Total Energy" include?
A: Total Energy combines Active Energy (calories burned through activity) and Resting Energy (basal metabolic rate) to match the total shown in the Health app.

🔧 Troubleshooting

Common Issues & Solutions

❌ "No data found" or data shows "N/A"
Solution: Check Health app permissions. Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → My Health Export and make sure the data types you want to export are enabled.
❌ Can't select certain metrics (grayed out)
Solution: Metrics beyond the 8 free options require the Pro upgrade. Tap the metric to see the upgrade prompt.
❌ Can't change the date range
Solution: Custom date ranges require the Pro upgrade. Free users can export the last 7 days only.
❌ "Free plan allows up to 10 exports per day"
Solution: You've reached the daily free export limit. Wait until tomorrow, or upgrade to Pro for unlimited exports.
❌ Export or sharing failed
Solution: Check that you have enough storage space on your device. For very large exports, try a shorter date range or fewer metrics.
❌ App crashes or freezes
Solution: Force-close the app (swipe up from the app switcher) and reopen it. If the problem continues, restart your iPhone.

💡 Tips for Best Results

  • Keep devices synced: Open the Health app regularly to sync data from your Apple Watch and other connected devices
  • Grant permissions up front: Allow access to all the metrics you might want to export – you can always deselect them later
  • Use summary for trends: Summary exports (one row per day) work great for spreadsheets and charts
  • Use raw for detail: Raw export (Pro) gives you every reading with timestamps – useful for detailed analysis or sharing specific data with your doctor
  • Check your units: Choose metric or imperial in the app's first-run setup (or in Settings) to match your preferred measurement system
  • Large exports take time: Exporting years of data for frequently-recorded metrics (like heart rate) can take a minute or two – watch the progress bar
