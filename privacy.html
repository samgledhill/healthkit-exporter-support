Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Introduction

My Health Export ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices when you use our iOS application.

Data Collection

🔒 We Do Not Collect Any Data

My Health Export does not collect, store, transmit or share any data from your device. This includes:

  • No health data
  • No personal information
  • No analytics or usage data
  • No crash reports
  • No device identifiers
  • No location data

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Health Data Privacy

Your Health Data Stays on Your Device

  • Read-Only Access: The app reads health data from the Apple Health app with your permission, but never writes or changes your Health data
  • Local Processing: All health data processing happens locally on your iPhone
  • No Transmission: Your health readings are never sent to our servers or any third parties
  • You Control Export: Health data only leaves your device when you choose to export and share a file yourself
  • Revoke Anytime: You can revoke HealthKit permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health

In-App Purchases

Purchases in My Health Export are processed through Apple's StoreKit framework. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple handles all purchase transactions according to their privacy policy.

The app stores only a local record that you have purchased Pro to unlock features. This record stays on your device and can be restored on other devices signed in to the same Apple ID.

Apple HealthKit

The app requests read-only access to health data types you choose to export. You control which data types the app can access through iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health. The app cannot access any health data you have not explicitly authorized.

All HealthKit data access is governed by Apple's HealthKit terms and privacy requirements.

Children's Privacy

My Health Export does not collect any data from anyone, including children. The app is suitable for all ages, subject to parental decisions about health data access.
