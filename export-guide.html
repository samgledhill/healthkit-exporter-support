How to Export Apple Health Data to CSV or Excel

A step-by-step guide to getting your health data into a spreadsheet

📥 Step 1: Download My Health Export

If you haven't already, download My Health Export from the App Store. The app is free for core metrics (steps, heart rate, weight, and sleep) covering the last 7 days.

🔓 Step 2: Grant Health App Permissions

  1. Open My Health Export on your iPhone
  2. When prompted, tap Allow to grant Health app access
  3. In the permission screen, turn on the toggle for each data type you want to export (e.g. Steps, Heart Rate, Sleep)
  4. Tap Allow in the top right corner
💡 Tip: You can always change permissions later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → My Health Export

📊 Step 3: Select Your Metrics and Date Range

  1. In the app, you'll see a list of available metrics. Each one you granted permission to will have a checkbox
  2. Tick the checkboxes for the metrics you want in your export
  3. If you have the Pro upgrade, you can also:
    • Change the date range to any period (tap the date selector at the top)
    • Enable "Export as Raw data" on any metric to get timestamped individual readings

💾 Step 4: Export to CSV

  1. Scroll to the bottom and tap Export as CSV
  2. The app will process your data (this may take a few seconds for large exports)
  3. When complete, the iOS share sheet will appear

📤 Step 5: Open in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers

Option A: Save to Files, then open on computer

  1. In the share sheet, tap Save to Files
  2. Choose a location (iCloud Drive works great for syncing to your computer)
  3. On your computer, open the file in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers

Option B: AirDrop to Mac

  1. In the share sheet, tap your Mac's name under AirDrop
  2. Accept the file on your Mac
  3. Open the CSV file in Excel, Numbers or your preferred spreadsheet app

Option C: Email to yourself

  1. In the share sheet, tap Mail
  2. Send the email to yourself
  3. Open the email on your computer and download the attachment

📈 Understanding Your CSV File

Summary Export (Default)

Your CSV file will have one row per day, with columns like:

  • Date: The day the data is from (YYYY-MM-DD format)
  • Steps: Total steps for that day
  • Heart Rate (bpm): Average heart rate
  • Weight (kg or lbs): Most recent weight recorded that day
  • Sleep (hours): Total sleep duration
💡 Tip: Each day runs from midnight to midnight in your local time zone

Raw Export (Pro Only)

If you enabled raw export, you'll also get a "detailed" file with individual readings:

  • Timestamp: ISO 8601 date and time (YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS)
  • Date: Just the date portion (YYYY-MM-DD)
  • Time: Just the time portion (HH:MM:SS)
  • Metric: Which health metric this reading is for
  • Value: The recorded value
  • Unit: The unit of measurement
  • Source: Which device or app recorded it (e.g. "Apple Watch", "iPhone")

🎯 What Can I Do With My Health Data CSV?

Analyze Trends

Create charts in Excel or Google Sheets to visualize your health trends over time. Track correlations between different metrics (like sleep and heart rate).

Share With Healthcare Providers

Bring a clean CSV file to doctor appointments instead of showing individual readings on your phone. Your healthcare provider can import it into their systems.

Use With AI Tools

Upload your health data CSV to AI assistants like ChatGPT or Claude for personalized insights and analysis. The structured format makes it easy for AI to understand.

Run Your Own Analysis

Use Python, R, or other data tools to perform statistical analysis, build models, or integrate your health data with other datasets.

Keep Personal Records

Maintain your own archive of health data in a format you can always open, regardless of what apps or services you use in the future.

❓ Common Questions

Q: Can I export multiple metrics at once?
Yes! Just tick multiple checkboxes before exporting. They'll all appear as columns in your CSV file.
Q: Why does Excel show dates wrong?
Some Excel versions auto-format dates. Right-click the column → Format Cells → Text to preserve the original format.
Q: How do I export more than 7 days of data?
The Pro upgrade (one-time purchase) unlocks any date range. You can export your entire health history.
Q: What's the difference between CSV and JSON?
CSV files open in Excel and spreadsheets. JSON is better for scripts, programming, and automation tools.
